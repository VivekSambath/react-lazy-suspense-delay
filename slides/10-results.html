☕Does ~200 ms matter? At ~107 new tabs a day, saving ~200 ms per widget adds up to ~20 seconds a day, or about 2 hours a year.

How it was measured: Chrome 154.0.8037.98 on Windows 11 (Intel i9-14900KF, 64 GB 6000 MT/s, T-FORCE TM8FPL2000G NVMe SSD). 4 cold new tabs per build; a busy 20-widget dashboard (incl. Gmail, Google Drive, Tasks, Spotify, an RSS reader and a pets game), 16 visible, signed out; plain grey wallpaper, entrance animation off. Times are read from trace screenshots, counted from when the tab started loading. The seven Pro widgets (Gmail, Calendar, Drive and others) were excluded because signed out they show a sign-in overlay. These numbers can't be compared with the headless ones.
